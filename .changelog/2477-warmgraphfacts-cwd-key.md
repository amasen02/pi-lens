- **`recordEntitySnapshotDiff` enforces its absolute-`filePath` invariant instead of folding `cwd` into the review-graph snapshot key (closes #2477)** — an earlier fix folded `cwd` into the entity-snapshot/`changedSymbols` FactStore key to close a hypothetical cross-project collision in `mcp/analyze.ts`'s long-lived warm `warmGraphFacts` store. That collision cannot reach `recordEntitySnapshotDiff` in production: its sole writer (`clients/dispatch/runners/tree-sitter.ts`) always passes `ctx.filePath`, which `createDispatchContext` resolves to an always-absolute path (the #2016 invariant), so two project roots sharing a relative spelling (`src/index.ts`) never produce the same key. Folding `cwd` in anyway broke the review-graph cascade instead: the writer's `ctx.cwd` is the file's nearest language-root, while the builder's cascade reader keys off the workspace cwd, and those diverge in a monorepo/cargo-workspace/go-module layout — the folded key the reader could never read back zeroed `graphChangedSymbolCount` on every build. `recordEntitySnapshotDiff` now enforces the absolute-path invariant instead: a non-absolute `filePath` (a caller regression, unreachable from the real writer) is rejected with a visible, bounded `review-graph-non-absolute-entity-path` degradation record and the diff is skipped, rather than either computing one under an unreachable folded key or silently diffing against the wrong project. No behavior change for the real, always-absolute production path.
